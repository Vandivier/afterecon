After Economics (AfterEcon) is one of several blogs from Dr. John Vandivier.

John is a programmer and author with a Ph.D. in economics from George Mason University.

John began blogging in 2010 during his undergraduate education at the University of Houston and continues through today.

John has expanded content creation beyond blogging to include video content and other media.

You can [support John's work through Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cw/johnvandivier), find [his other writing here](/posts/2026-05-17-where-to-read-newer-writings), and find his [social media links here](https://vandivier.github.io/not-johns-linktree/).
